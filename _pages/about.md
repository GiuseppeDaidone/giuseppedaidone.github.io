# Short Bio

👋 Hi! I am Giuseppe Daidone, PhD student in Cybersecurity at Sapienza University of Rome and Luiss University.

My research interests include adversarial machine learning techniques for designing secure AI models, multimedia forensics for detecting manipulated digital content, and deep learning based techniques for malware analysis.

# Long Formal Bio

Giuseppe Daidone is a PhD student in Joint Cybersecurity Program at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://www.uniroma1.it/) and [Luiss University](https://www.luiss.it/).

He has achieved a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Engineering at the [University of Bergamo](https://www.unibg.it/) in 2023, with the thesis *Exploration of vulnerabilities found in Electron applications*, under the supervision of [Prof. S. Paraboschi](https://cs.unibg.it/parabosc/) and [Seclab](https://seclab.unibg.it/people/). He has achieved a Master's Degree in Engineering in Computer Science at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://www.uniroma1.it/) in 2025, specializing in Cybersecurity and Deep Learning fields intersection, with the thesis *Adversarial Attacks on Convolutional and Transformer Architectures for Image-based Malware Detection and Classification Tasks*, aligned with the objectives of the ArIS (Artificial Intelligence Sandboxing) PNRM Project of [NAVARM](https://www.difesa.it/sgd-dna/staff/dt/navarm/index/24817.html) (Naval Weaponry Direction of Ministry of Defence) and Telsy SPA, under the supervision of [Prof. I. Amerini](https://sites.google.com/diag.uniroma1.it/ireneamerini), co-supervision of [Prof. L. Querzoni](https://sites.google.com/diag.uniroma1.it/querzoni/home-page), and co-examining of [Prof. S. Bonomi](https://bonomi.diag.uniroma1.it/).

His research interests include (but not limited to):
-   Adversarial machine learning techniques for designing secure Al models.
-   Multimedia forensics for detecting manipulated digital content.
-   Deep learning based techniques for malware analysis.

His research project focuses on *Watermarking and Proactive Defense against Malicious Generative Al* in collaboration with [National Cybersecurity Agency](https://www.acn.gov.it/portale/home) (ACN), President of the Council of Ministers. His PhD journey is supervised by [Prof. I. Masi](https://iacopomasi.github.io/) at [OmnAl Lab](https://omnai.di.uniroma1.it/), Department of Computer Science, Sapienza, and [Prof. I. Amerini](https://sites.google.com/diag.uniroma1.it/ireneamerini) at [ALCOR Lab](https://alcorlab.diag.uniroma1.it/), Department of Computer, Control and Management Engineering, Sapienza.

Since 2026, he is Member of the [Italian Intelligence Society](https://www.socint.org/) (SOCINT) for intelligence studies. Moreover, he actively participate in important national security, cybersecurity and intelligence events, under patronage of: President of the Council of Ministers, Chamber of Deputies, Ministry of Defence, Ministry of the Interior, European Commission.
